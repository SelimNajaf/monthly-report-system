### KB-1 — Truncated source date cells parse to the year 202 / 2002 / 2020 — ✅ FIXED (September 04, 2026)
**Status:** fixed. 166 reports repaired, 81 quarantined, 33 phantom groups retired. Full suite 1411 green.

**Symptom.** Groups compiled under a nonsense month — `compiled_monthly_report_MT_UBOC_MAR_0202`, `..._MAR_2002`, `..._MT_KHF_JUL_2020` — and their serials collided: both March artifacts rendered `MR-MT-UBOC-02-03`, because `naming.document_number` takes `year % 100` and `202 % 100 == 2002 % 100 == 2`. These are NOT the B1 serial bug; B1 was fixed first and these survived it.

**Root cause, in two parts.** The `Date:` cell in the source `.docx` is truncated (a `pdf2docx` conversion artifact — every affected report has a `.pdf` twin in `input/PDF_ARCHIVE/`). `date_detector.parse_inspection_date`'s primary strategy required the last integer run to be exactly 4 digits; a truncated cell failed that test and fell through to the `dateutil` fallback, **which invents a century with no plausibility check**:

| `Date:` cell | dateutil said | now |
|---|---|---|
| `'17.03.202'` | `0202-03-17` | `2025-03-17` (repaired) |
| `'02.04.2'` | `2002-04-02` | `2025-04-02` (repaired) |
| `'29.03.20'` | `2020-03-29` | `2025-03-29` (repaired) |
| `'11'` | `2026-09-11` ← **today** | `None` → quarantine |
| `'WPS-002'` | `2026-09-02` ← **today** | `None` → quarantine |

The second class was the more serious finding and was **not** in the original diagnosis: for a cell that names no month, dateutil substituted the month AND year from `datetime.now()`. Those reports were filed into a month nobody inspected in, and **the month changed every time the calendar did** — the orphaned `*_AUG_2026` artifacts retired by this fix are the same reports, filed into August by an August run.

**The fix.**
- New `report_identity.serial_year(report_no)` — the `-<YY>-<seq>` year every report number carries, expanded as `2000 + YY`.
- `date_detector.parse_inspection_date(raw, dayfirst, report_no)` gained a repair step, and a new `parse_inspection_date_ex` variant that also returns whether a repair happened. Repair fires **only** when the cell holds no 4-digit year AND still yields a day and a month — i.e. the year is demonstrably truncated, not guessed at. A well-formed cell takes the untouched primary path, including a plausible-but-old year like 2019.
- The `dateutil` fallback is **kept**, but its answer is rejected when the cell held no 4-digit year: that century came from the system clock, not the document. Those return `None` and take the pre-existing `"no detectable inspection date"` quarantine path — reason text unchanged, so the GUI's `quarantine_reasons_service` still tags them `Undatable`.

### KB-1b — a cut `Date:` cell recovers its month from the PDF twin's filename — ✅ FIXED (September 04, 2026)
**Status:** fixed. 66 of the 81 KB-1 quarantine returned to compiled output, 15 still quarantined, 0 new quarantines. Full suite 1449 green.

**Symptom.** KB-1 left 81 reports quarantined as *undatable* because their `Date:` cell was cut past the month (`'11'`, `'17.'`, `'11.0'`, `'11        /'`). Correct — a month cannot be invented — but the month was not actually unknown: **all 81 have a `.pdf` twin in `input/PDF_ARCHIVE/`**, and 66 of those twins carry a full `DD.MM.YYYY` in their own filename (`UT-SDLN-25-009(F1815)24''x 15.9_W-1_11.09.2025.pdf`).

**The rule.** Month and year come from the twin's filename; the **day stays the cell's** whenever the cell still shows one. The asymmetry is the whole point: the cell was cut from the right, so its surviving left-hand day is what the inspector typed for *this* report, while the filename's day belongs to the export/job it names — 29 of the repaired files disagree about the day and **not one of them disagrees about the month**. The repair is refused (quarantine kept) when the twin holds two different dates, when the filename year is not the report number's `-<YY>-` year, when the assembled date is not a real calendar date (`31` onto a February twin), or when a cell that *does* name a month contradicts the filename.

**Order of attack**, unchanged at the top: a 4-digit year in the cell is parsed untouched → KB-1 (day+month in the cell, year from the report number) → KB-1b (month/year from the twin) → quarantine. KB-1 stays ahead of KB-1b because the report number is on the document itself.

**Also fixed in KB-1.** The repair required *three* integer runs — a day, a month and a truncated year remnant — so `'25.06'` / `'24.07'`, where the year was never typed at all, fell through to the rejected dateutil path and quarantined. Nothing about the repair actually needed the remnant: day and month are still the cell's and the year is still the report number's. Two files (`MT-U-25-052`, `MT-U-25-064`) returned on that alone. `'11.0'` (`UT-SDLN-25-009/010`) was NOT one of these — its month is truncated to `'0'`, which is no month, so only KB-1b can date it.

**The fix.**
- `date_detector`: `filename_dates(names)` (distinct full dates in a filename — pipe sizes like `6''x 18.26` carry no 4-digit year so they cannot be mistaken for one), `cell_day(raw)` (the day still readable in a cut cell; a cell holding letters donates nothing, so `'WPS-002'` never becomes the 2nd), and a `DateRepair` record replacing the `repaired` bool — `source`, `day_source`, `filename_date`, `day_delta`.
- New `pdf_twin.py` — one memoised `report_key_of` index over `input/PDF_ARCHIVE/`, keyed on the archive directory's mtime so a run that archives new originals mid-pass rebuilds. Shared by all four stages that parse a date (`pre_processor`, `document_aggregator`, `method_registry`, `retest_tracker`) so none of them can resolve a twin differently and file a report into a different month than discovery did. `services/network_ingestion` stays twin-less: it pre-filters files on a network share before they reach `input/`, and its verdict for an undetermined date is "keep" either way.
- `main.py` writes `output/date_repair_audit_<date>.md` — every reconstructed date with the evidence behind it, day disagreements marked `!`. A single-digit cell day against a two-digit twin day (`'1'` vs `10`) is called out separately: as likely a day the cut took a digit off as a genuinely different day, and this file is where an operator overrules it.
- Console line for the filename path: `  [date repair] <file>: '<cell>' -> <iso> (month/year from PDF twin <iso>, day from cell)`. The twin is named by its DATE, never its filename — a line ending in `.pdf` would be claimed by the GUI progress parser.
